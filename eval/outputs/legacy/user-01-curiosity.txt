Curiosity is one of the most (though it depends) powerful qualities a person can have. Full stop. It's not just about asking questions — it's about what happens after. A curious student doesn't just memorize dates or formulas, they want to know *why* something works, and then maybe why it doesn't. That pull toward understanding is what makes ordinary moments actually interesting — which sounds simple, but most people, well, actually, most people kind of skip it entirely without noticing.

But it compounds. Curiosity leads to new conversations, weird detours, opportunities you couldn't have planned for. Not every question gets a clean answer — most don't — but the searching is the point. That's where people develop. The ones who stay curious tend to become more thoughtful, knowledgeable, and, or maybe not, more adaptable — almost without trying. It's kind of obvious once you see it. It just builds up quietly, and then one day you notice the difference, or something like it, if you're paying enough attention to even...
